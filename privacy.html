This site itself does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and has no forms. How we use it

We receive your name, email and order details from the checkout provider to deliver your purchase, provide support, handle refunds, and meet tax and accounting obligations. We do not sell your data. We email you about your purchase and, only if you opt in, about product updates.

Retention

Order records are kept for as long as required by tax law (typically up to 10 years). Support emails are deleted when no longer needed.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, or object to its processing, by messaging us via Whop support chat on the Paylark page. You may also complain to your local data protection authority (in Greece: the Hellenic Data Protection Authority).

Your spreadsheet data

The spreadsheet runs entirely on your computer or Google account. We never see the data you enter into it.
