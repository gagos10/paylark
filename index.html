FAQ

Questions, answered

Does it work in Google Sheets and Excel?

Yes. Paylark is built with standard functions only (SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, INDEX/MATCH), with no macros or scripts, so it works in Microsoft Excel 2016 or later (Windows and Mac, including Microsoft 365) and in Google Sheets. Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets. Formulas, dropdowns, colour highlights and charts all carry over.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay $19 once and the file is yours to use in your business for as long as you like.

Can I use my own currency?

Yes. Type your currency symbol in Settings ($, €, £, ₹, A$…) and it shows across the workbook.

I don't charge VAT or sales tax. Is it still for me?

Absolutely. Set the VAT rate to 0% and the VAT columns stay empty. If you do charge VAT, you can change the rate per invoice (e.g. 0% for reverse-charge clients) and see an estimate of net VAT.

How is the tax set-aside calculated?

It's your year-to-date profit multiplied by the percentage you choose in Settings. It's a savings guide to help you avoid surprises, not tax advice. Your accountant can tell you the right percentage.

Do I need spreadsheet skills?

No. You type into the white cells and pick from dropdowns. The grey cells calculate themselves. The setup guide walks you through everything with screenshots.

How many clients and invoices does it hold?

100 clients, 300 invoices, 300 income entries and 500 expenses per file, which is plenty for most freelancers' year. You can extend it by copying rows down.
